Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

This OAuth client ("rclone") is a personal tool used only by its owner to copy and sync the owner's own Google Drive files with rclone.

  • It is not offered to the public, and no other users are expected to authorize it.
  • Google user data is accessed only by rclone running on the owner's own machines, only to transfer the owner's own files.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising or AI training.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
